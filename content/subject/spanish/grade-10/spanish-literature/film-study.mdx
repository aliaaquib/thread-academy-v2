## Key ideas

### Writing about film

Discuss dirección (direction), fotografía, montaje (editing) and actuaciones (performances). Structure essays with a clear thesis: introduce the director's intent, analyse two or three key scenes with technical vocabulary, and conclude on the film's message.

### Analysing poetry

Comment on imagery (imágenes), sound patterns (rima, ritmo, aliteración) and structure (estrofas, versos). Link technique to meaning: Lorca's moon imagery in Romancero gitano evokes both beauty and death — always connect device to effect.

**Key term — el plano:** A film shot — el primer plano (close-up), la banda sonora (soundtrack), el montaje (editing).

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Ignoring context** A film's historical and cultural context (e.g. post-Franco Spain for Almodóvar) shapes its meaning — mention it.
  - **Quoting without comment** Every quotation needs explanation — say what the technique is and what effect it has.
</ImportantNote>
