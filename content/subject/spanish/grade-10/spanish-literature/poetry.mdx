## Key ideas

### Analysing poetry

Comment on imagery (imágenes), sound patterns (rima, ritmo, aliteración) and structure (estrofas, versos). Link technique to meaning: Lorca's moon imagery in Romancero gitano evokes both beauty and death — always connect device to effect.

### Reading authentic texts

Do not translate word by word — read for gist first, tolerate ambiguity, and use cognates to guess. Keep a vocabulary log of recurring words. Re-reading with a dictionary the second time round deepens understanding enormously.

**Key term — la metáfora:** Metaphor. Lorca's verse is rich in metaphor and símbolo (symbolism) — always link the device to its effect.

<WorkedExample title="Analysing a metaphor">
  Lorca presents the moon as a cold, watchful presence. Write a two-sentence analysis in Spanish.

  Identify the device: personification and metaphor of the moon.
  Link it to theme: death and fate in the Romancero gitano.
  Draft: Lorca personifica la luna como una presencia fría que observa a los personajes, lo que refuerza la inevitabilidad del destino.
  Extend: Esta imagen recurrente convierte la luna en símbolo de la muerte.

  **Answer:** Lorca personifica la luna como una presencia fría que observa a los personajes, lo que refuerza la inevitabilidad del destino. Esta imagen recurrente convierte la luna en símbolo de la muerte.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Retelling the plot instead of analysing** Examiners reward analysis of how meaning is created, not summaries of what happens.
  - **Quoting without comment** Every quotation needs explanation — say what the technique is and what effect it has.
</ImportantNote>
