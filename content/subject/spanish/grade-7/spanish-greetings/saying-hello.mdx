First impressions count in every language. This chapter gives you the essential social toolkit: greetings for every time of day, confident self-introductions, and the polite phrases that open doors across the Spanish-speaking world.

This lesson focuses on Saying Hello: learn hola and greetings for different times of day.

<Definition term="Saying Hello">
  Learn hola and greetings for different times of day.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Greetings change with the day

Use buenos días in the morning, buenas tardes in the afternoon, and buenas noches in the evening — note that noches covers both 'evening' and 'night' as a greeting. ¿Qué tal? (How's it going?) and ¿Cómo estás? (How are you?) keep conversations friendly.

### Introducing yourself

Me llamo… states your name, and Tengo… años gives your age — note Spanish uses 'I have' for age: Tengo diez años (I am ten). Soy de… says where you are from: Soy de Londres (I am from London).

**Key term — hola:** Hello — the universal greeting, friendly at any time of day and in any Spanish-speaking country.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Saying Hello">
  How do you say 'hello' in Spanish?

  ¡Hola!

  **Answer:** ¡Hola!
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying 'yo soy diez años'** Age uses tener, not ser: Tengo diez años (I have ten years).
  - **Using 'buenas noches' only as goodbye** It also means 'good evening' as a greeting when you arrive somewhere at night.
</ImportantNote>
