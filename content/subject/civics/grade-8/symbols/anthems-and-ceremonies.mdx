## Key ideas

### Ceremonies show respect

When people stand for an anthem or observe a silence, they show respect for shared memories and values. Taking part respectfully, even when the ceremony is not from your own background, is a sign of good manners and kindness.

### Symbols carry big meanings

A symbol is something small that stands for something much bigger. A heart shape can stand for love, and a flag can stand for a whole country. Because symbols are easy to recognise, they help people feel connected to shared ideas.

**Key term — anthem:** A special song that represents a country or group. National anthems are played at official ceremonies and international events.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Anthems and Ceremonies">
  What is a national anthem?

  It is a special song that represents a country and its people. People sing it at ceremonies and sports events to show pride and togetherness.

  **Answer:** It is a special song that represents a country and its people. People sing it at ceremonies and sports events to show pride and togetherness.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Ignoring the words of an anthem** Anthems are poems set to music about a country's hopes and history. Listening to the words helps you understand what people are proud of. Singing without thinking misses the meaning.
  - **Forgetting to be respectful** Talking, laughing or fidgeting during a ceremony can upset people for whom it is meaningful. Standing quietly and listening shows respect even if you are still learning. Good behaviour during ceremonies matters.
</ImportantNote>
