<Definition term="Giving Evidence">
  Learn how to back up what you believe with evidence others can check.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### A reason is not just a feeling

'Because I feel like it' is a feeling, not a reason. A real reason gives someone else a way to check whether the belief makes sense.

### Changing your mind is brave

Many children worry that changing their mind means losing. In fact, giving up a weak belief for a stronger one shows you care more about truth than about winning.

**Key term — Evidence:** Information that supports a belief, such as something you saw, measured, or read from a trustworthy source.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Giving Evidence">
  Is 'because I want to' a good reason? Why not?

  No: it is a feeling, not a reason others can check or test.

  **Answer:** No: it is a feeling, not a reason others can check or test.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying 'because I said so' or 'because I want to'** These are not reasons: they give nobody any way to check your thinking. Try adding evidence someone else could look at.
  - **Changing your mind only when pressured** Change your mind because the reasons are good, not because friends pressure you. Truth matters more than fitting in.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Give one piece of evidence for the belief 'It rained last night'." hint="What would you see outside?">
    The ground is wet, or there are puddles on the path.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Imagine your friend shows you proof you were wrong about a film's ending. What should you do?" hint="Is changing your mind weak or brave?">
    Change your mind: admitting you were wrong when the evidence is good is brave and honest.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Which is stronger: a belief with reasons or a belief without reasons? Why?" hint="Which one can be checked?">
    A belief with reasons, because the reasons can be checked and discussed.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is a reason?" hint="Finish the sentence: 'I believe it because...'">
    A reason is a fact or idea that supports a belief, often given after the word 'because'.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
