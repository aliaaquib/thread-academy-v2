## Key ideas

### The causative and keigo

Causative: Group 2 verbs drop る and add させる (たべる → たべさせる); Group 1 verbs change -u to -a and add せる (かく → かかせる). It means 'make' or 'let' depending on context. Keigo has two directions: 尊敬語 (sonkeigo) raises the other person — いく → いらっしゃる (irassharu); 謙譲語 (kenjōgo) lowers yourself — いく → まいる (mairu). Never use humble forms for the other person's actions — that reverses the respect.

### Forming the passive

Group 2 (ichidan) verbs: drop る, add られる — たべる → たべられる (taberareru, to be eaten). Group 1 (godan) verbs: change the final -u sound to -a and add れる — かく → かかれる (kakareru, to be written), のむ → のまれる (nomareru, to be drunk). The agent takes に: 母にしかられた (haha ni shikarareta, I was scolded by my mother). Japanese also has the 'suffering passive' for unwelcome events: 雨に降られた (ame ni furareta, I was rained on — and suffered for it).

**Key term — そんけいご (sonkeigo):** Honorific language that elevates the other person: いう → おっしゃる (ossharu, to say).

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Honorific and Humble Language">
  Your guest is going out. Which verb shows proper respect — いらっしゃる or まいる?

  いらっしゃる — the honorific form. まいる is humble, for your own actions.

  **Answer:** いらっしゃる — the honorific form. まいる is humble, for your own actions.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Reversing keigo direction** Using the humble もうす (mōsu) for the other person's words ✗ — humble language is only for yourself and your in-group. The other person's speech takes おっしゃる ✓.
  - **Forming the Group 1 passive like Group 2** かく → かくられる ✗. Change the final -u to -a first: かく → かかれる ✓ (kakareru, to be written).
</ImportantNote>

  <PracticeItem question="What is the honorific (尊敬語) form of いう (to say)?" hint="It starts with お.">
    おっしゃる (ossharu).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Give the passive form of のむ (to drink)." hint="Group 1: change -u to -a, add れる.">

  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Give the causative form of たべる." hint="Group 2: drop る, add させる.">

  </PracticeItem>
