<Definition term="The Moon Landing">
  How astronauts first walked on the Moon in 1969.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Why we remember

We remember the Moon landing because it showed what humans can achieve together. People celebrate or commemorate events that matter to them, and remembering helps nations understand who they are.

### One event can change everything

Before 1666, London was a crowded city of wooden houses. After the Great Fire burned most of it down, the city was rebuilt in brick and stone with wider streets. A single event, lasting just four days, reshaped the capital for centuries.

**Key term — Achievement:** Something difficult that someone succeeds in doing. Landing on the Moon was an amazing achievement.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: The Moon Landing">
  In which year did humans first walk on the Moon?

  1969.

  **Answer:** 1969.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Thinking the Moon landing was one person's work** Thousands of scientists, engineers and workers made the Moon landing possible. Neil Armstrong walked on the Moon, but a huge team got him there.
  - **Thinking the fire was the end of London** London was rebuilt quickly in brick and stone, and the new buildings, including St Paul's Cathedral, made it grander than before.
</ImportantNote>
