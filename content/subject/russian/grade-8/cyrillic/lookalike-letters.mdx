<Definition term="Look-Alike Letters">
  Tell apart tricky pairs like Ш (sha) and Щ (shcha), И (i) and Й (short y), Е (ye) and Ё (yo).
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Confusable pairs

Several letters look alike but sound different: Ш (sha — 'sh') versus Щ (shcha — a softer, longer 'shch'); Е (ye) versus Ё (yo); И (ee) versus Й (short y); В (v) versus Б (b). A classic exam trap is misreading мышь (mysh' — mouse), so train your eye on the small details — the tail on Щ, the dots on Ё.

### The two signs that are not sounds

Ь (the soft sign) palatalises the consonant before it: your tongue rises toward the palate, as in мать (mat' — mother) versus мат (mat — checkmate). Ъ (the hard sign) does the opposite job: it keeps a prefix and the following vowel apart, so съесть (s"yest' — to eat up) is pronounced 's-yest' with a clear break, not blended.

**Key term — й (i krátkoye) — short i:** A quick 'y' glide, as in май (mai — May). It turns the vowel и into a diphthong ending: compare и (i — and) with ой (oy).

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Look-Alike Letters">
  Which letter starts щука (pike): Ш or Щ?

  Щ: щука (shchúka — pike).

  **Answer:** Щ: щука (shchúka — pike).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Reading Щ as Ш** Щ (shcha) is softer and longer than Ш (sha): щука (shchúka — pike) is not шука. Listen for the extra breath in Щ.
  - **Sounding out handwriting letter by letter** A cursive т looks like a Latin m — reading letter by letter produces nonsense. Read whole words and use context instead.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="What does a handwritten т look like?" hint="It looks like a Latin letter it is not.">
    Like a Latin m — so read whole words, not letter by letter.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What does the soft sign Ь do?" hint="It has no sound of its own.">
    It softens (palatalises) the consonant before it, as in мать (mat' — mother) versus мат (mat — checkmate).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Where does the hard sign Ъ appear?" hint="Think of под + езд.">
    Between a prefix and the vowels я, е, ё or ю, forcing a small pause, as in подъезд (pod"yézd — entrance).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Read всё and все — what is the difference?" hint="Look for the two dots.">
    Всё (vsyo) means 'everything'; все (vse) means 'everyone, all'. The dots on Ё change the meaning.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
