## Key ideas

### Eleven time zones of etiquette

Russia spans eleven time zones, from Kaliningrad to Kamchatka, and regional traditions vary — but some rules are national: bring flowers in odd numbers (even numbers are for funerals), remove your shoes indoors and accept the offered тапочки (tápochki — slippers), and never shake hands across a threshold. The баня (bánya — steam bath) remains a beloved ritual of heat, steam and friendship.

### Хлебосольство: the cult of hospitality

The traditional welcome offers хлеб-соль (khleb-sol' — bread and salt), and the virtue of хлебосольство (khlebosól'stvo — generous hospitality) still rules: guests must eat, refusing is almost rude, and the table groans under salads like оливье (oliv'yé — Olivier salad). Tea — чай (chai) — is served constantly; 'have some tea' really means 'stay a while'.

**Key term — Новый год (Nóvyy god) — New Year:** Russia's biggest winter holiday, celebrated on the night of 31 December with a decorated ёлка (yólka — fir tree), Дед Мороз (Ded Moróz — Father Frost) and the Kremlin chimes at midnight.

<WorkedExample title="Bringing flowers correctly">
  You are visiting a Russian friend's mother. How many flowers do you bring, and why?

  Russian etiquette demands an odd number of flowers for happy occasions.
  Even numbers of flowers are reserved for funerals and mourning.
  Choose 3, 5 or 7 — five roses is classic and generous.
  Hand them over with a smile: she will notice that you knew the rule.

  **Answer:** Bring an odd number of flowers — five roses is classic. Чётное количество (chyótnoye kolíchestvo — an even number) is for funerals; нечётное (nechyótnoye — odd) is for celebrations.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Bringing an even number of flowers** Two or four roses suggest a funeral. Always bring an odd number — 3, 5 or 7 — for a celebration.
  - **Keeping your shoes on indoors** Hosts offer тапочки (tápochki — slippers) for a reason: walking indoors in outdoor shoes is considered disrespectful.
</ImportantNote>
