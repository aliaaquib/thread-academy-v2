</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Participles and gerunds

Active present participles add -ущ- or -ащ- to the stem: читающий (chitáyushchiy — reading, who is reading). Past passive participles like прочитанный (prochítannyy — having been read) compress a whole clause: прочитанная книга (prochítannaya kníga — the book that has been read). Gerunds (деепричастия (deyeprichástiya)) describe a secondary action: читая (chitáya — while reading), прочитав (prochitáv — having read) — Читая газету, он пил чай (chitáya gazétu, on pil chay — while reading the newspaper, he drank tea).

### Choosing aspect

Use the imperfective for processes, habits and repeated actions: Я читал книгу вчера (ya chitál knígu vcherá — I was reading the book yesterday). Use the perfective for single completed results: Я прочитал книгу (ya prochitál knígu — I have finished the book). After уже (uzhé — already) the perfective is almost always right: Я уже сделал домашнее задание (ya uzhé sdélal domáshneye zadániye — I have already done my homework).

**Key term — причастие (prichástiye) — participle:** A verb form acting as an adjective: читающий (chitáyushchiy — reading, who is reading), прочитанный (prochítannyy — read, that has been read).

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Participles in Action">
  Form the active present participle of читать.

  Читающий (chitáyushchiy — reading, who is reading).

  **Answer:** Читающий (chitáyushchiy — reading, who is reading).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Using the imperfective after уже** Уже (uzhé — already) signals completion: Я уже прочитал (ya uzhé prochitál — I have already read), not Я уже читал (which suggests 'I used to read').
  - **Using идти for a habit** Я иду в школу каждый день is wrong — a repeated trip needs ходить: Я хожу в школу каждый день (ya khozhú f shkólu kázhdyy den' — I go to school every day).
</ImportantNote>
