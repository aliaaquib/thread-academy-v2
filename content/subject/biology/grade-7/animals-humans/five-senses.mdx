Your body is a remarkable machine: it senses the world, holds itself upright, and repairs itself while you sleep. In this chapter you will explore the five senses, discover what your skeleton does for you, learn how to eat well, and find out the simple habits that keep humans healthy.

This lesson focuses on The Five Senses: explore sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch and the body parts that carry them.

## Key ideas

### The five senses

Humans detect the world with five senses: sight (eyes), hearing (ears), smell (nose), taste (tongue) and touch (skin). Each sense has a specialised organ. Smell and taste work together, which is why food tastes bland when your nose is blocked with a cold.

### What the skeleton does

The skeleton has three jobs: it supports the body and gives it shape, it protects delicate organs (the skull protects the brain, the ribs protect the heart and lungs), and it works with muscles attached to bones to produce movement. Babies are born with about 300 bones, some of which fuse together as they grow.

**Key term — sense organ:** A body part specialised for detecting the surroundings, such as the eyes for sight or the skin for touch.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: The Five Senses">
  Name the five senses and the organ used for each.

  Sight – eyes; hearing – ears; smell – nose; taste – tongue; touch – skin.

  **Answer:** Sight – eyes; hearing – ears; smell – nose; taste – tongue; touch – skin.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Confusing taste and smell** The tongue detects taste, but much of what we call flavour comes from smell detected by the nose.
  - **Saying the skull protects the heart** The skull protects the brain. The ribs protect the heart and lungs.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Why does food taste bland when you have a cold?" hint="Which two senses work together?">
    Because smell and taste work together to create flavour, and a blocked nose stops you smelling the food properly.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Give two jobs of the skeleton." hint="Think about protection and shape.">
    Any two of: supports the body and gives it shape; protects delicate organs such as the brain, heart and lungs; works with muscles to allow movement.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name the four main food groups in a balanced diet." hint="Fruit and vegetables are one group.">
    Fruit and vegetables, carbohydrates, protein, and dairy (with small amounts of fats and oils).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Give two reasons why exercise keeps you healthy." hint="Think about muscles and the heart.">
    Any two of: strengthens muscles and bones; keeps the heart healthy; helps maintain a healthy weight; improves mood and sleep.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
