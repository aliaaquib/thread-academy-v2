## Key ideas

### Staying healthy

A balanced diet gives the body energy and the nutrients it needs to grow and repair. Regular exercise strengthens muscles, bones and the heart. Good hygiene, such as washing hands before eating and brushing teeth twice a day, stops germs spreading and protects teeth. Enough sleep lets the body and brain recover.

### The five senses

Humans detect the world with five senses: sight (eyes), hearing (ears), smell (nose), taste (tongue) and touch (skin). Each sense has a specialised organ. Smell and taste work together, which is why food tastes bland when your nose is blocked with a cold.

**Key term — balanced diet:** Eating the right amounts of different food groups, including fruit and vegetables, carbohydrates, protein and dairy, to stay healthy.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Staying Healthy">
  Give two reasons why exercise keeps you healthy.

  Any two of: strengthens muscles and bones; keeps the heart healthy; helps maintain a healthy weight; improves mood and sleep.

  **Answer:** Any two of: strengthens muscles and bones; keeps the heart healthy; helps maintain a healthy weight; improves mood and sleep.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Thinking bones are dead and dry** Bones are living tissue with a blood supply. They grow, repair themselves when broken, and need calcium and exercise to stay strong.
  - **Saying the skull protects the heart** The skull protects the brain. The ribs protect the heart and lungs.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Why should you wash your hands before eating?" hint="What might be on your hands?">
    To remove germs picked up from surfaces, which could otherwise be transferred to food and cause illness.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name the five senses and the organ used for each." hint="Sight uses the eyes.">
    Sight – eyes; hearing – ears; smell – nose; taste – tongue; touch – skin.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Give two jobs of the skeleton." hint="Think about protection and shape.">
    Any two of: supports the body and gives it shape; protects delicate organs such as the brain, heart and lungs; works with muscles to allow movement.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Why does food taste bland when you have a cold?" hint="Which two senses work together?">
    Because smell and taste work together to create flavour, and a blocked nose stops you smelling the food properly.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
