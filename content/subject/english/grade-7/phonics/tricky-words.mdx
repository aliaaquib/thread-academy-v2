## Key ideas

### Some words break the rules

Many common words cannot be sounded out, such as said, was and the. These tricky words need to be learned by sight, and practising them little and often works best.

### Sounds before letter names

Always learn the sound a letter makes, not its name. The letter s says sss, not ess. Sounds can be pushed together into words; letter names get in the way of blending.

**Key term — phoneme:** The smallest unit of sound in a word. The word cat has three phonemes: c, a and t.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Tricky Words">
  Is the a tricky word?

  Yes — the does not follow the normal rules, so it must be learned by sight.

  **Answer:** Yes — the does not follow the normal rules, so it must be learned by sight.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying letter names instead of sounds** Say sss, a, t — not ess, ay, tee. Letter names cannot be blended into words.
  - **Guessing from the first letter** Always read every sound in the word. Guessing from the first letter causes mistakes like reading horse as house.
</ImportantNote>
