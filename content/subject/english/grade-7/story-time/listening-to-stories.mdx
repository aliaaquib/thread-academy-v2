Stories are how we make sense of the world. In Story Time we listen carefully, meet wonderful characters and learn how stories fit together. Soon you will be able to retell your favourite stories all by yourself.

This lesson focuses on Listening to Stories: practise sitting, listening and following a story from start to finish.

<Definition term="Listening to Stories">
  Practise sitting, listening and following a story from start to finish.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Listening is a skill

Good listeners sit still, look at the storyteller and keep the story in their heads. You can show you are listening by joining in with repeated phrases, such as roaring along with the giant. Listening well is the first step to understanding stories.

### Talk about how characters feel

Stories come alive when we think about feelings: a character might feel brave, scared, cross or kind. Asking how you would feel puts you inside the story and helps you understand why characters do what they do.

**Key term — ending:** How the story finishes. Good endings solve the problem or surprise the listener.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Listening to Stories">
  Which word helps you start telling the beginning of a story?

  First.

  **Answer:** First.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Forgetting to name the characters** Say who the story is about at the start, for example: This story is about a little duckling.
  - **Only remembering the ending** Practise saying one thing about the beginning and one thing about the middle before you reach the end.
</ImportantNote>
