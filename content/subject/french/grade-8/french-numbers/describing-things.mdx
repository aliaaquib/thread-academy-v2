<Definition term="Describing Things">
  Put colours with nouns: la voiture rouge.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Colour agreement

Adjectives match gender and number: un chat noir, une fleur noire, des chats noirs. Colours derived from nouns (orange, marron) usually do not change: des chaussures marron. But rose, mauve and pourpre are exceptions that do agree.

### Word order

Colours follow the noun: une voiture bleue (a blue car). Short common adjectives (petit, grand, joli) come before it: un petit chien noir.

**Key term — rouge:** Red. Colours agree with the noun: un ballon rouge, une pomme rouge, des ballons rouges.

<WorkedExample title="Translating 'two green cars'">
  Translate 'two green cars' into French.

  'Two' = deux.
  'Cars' = voitures (feminine plural).
  'Green' = vert, which must become feminine plural: vertes.
  The colour follows the noun: deux voitures vertes.

  **Answer:** Deux voitures vertes.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Making 'marron' agree** Des yeux marron stays unchanged — noun-derived colours do not agree (except rose, mauve and pourpre).
  - **Saying 'septante' in France** In France it is soixante-dix — septante is Belgian and Swiss usage.
</ImportantNote>
