## Key ideas

### Rituals of daily life

Meals structure the day: a light petit-déjeuner, a substantial déjeuner, and dîner around 8pm. La bise greets friends; politeness (bonjour, s'il vous plaît) is non-negotiable. Sundays remain family-centred, with many shops closed.

### Fêtes and heritage

From the Festival de Cannes to local fêtes de village, France celebrates culture intensely. Traditions like la galette des rois (Epiphany) and le 14 juillet express republican and regional identity — heritage (le patrimoine) is fiercely protected.

**Key term — la bise:** The cheek-kiss greeting — one to four kisses depending on the region. A key social ritual among friends and family.

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Calling French culture 'just France'** The francophonie spans Africa, the Caribbean and beyond — mention it.
  - **Generalising about 'the French'** France is regionally diverse — Parisian habits are not universal.
</ImportantNote>
