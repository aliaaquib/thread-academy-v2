<Definition term="Being Polite">
  Use s'il vous plaît, merci and tu/vous correctly.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Tu or vous?

Use tu with friends, family and children; vous with adults you do not know, teachers and in shops. Using tu with a stranger can seem rude — when in doubt, use vous. Compare s'il te plaît (informal) with s'il vous plaît (formal).

### Bonjour and beyond

Bonjour works until evening, when bonsoir takes over — bonsoir covers both 'good evening' and 'good night' as a greeting. Salut is for peers; bonjour is the safe default with adults. Add Comment ça va? (How's it going?) to sound natural.

**Key term — merci:** Thank you. Merci beaucoup adds warmth; de rien means 'you're welcome'.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Being Polite">
  What is the informal way to say 'please'?

  S'il te plaît (to friends); s'il vous plaît to adults.

  **Answer:** S'il te plaît (to friends); s'il vous plaît to adults.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying 'je suis neuf ans'** Age uses avoir: J'ai neuf ans (I have nine years).
  - **Using 'salut' with a teacher** Salut is informal — say Bonjour, madame / monsieur to adults.
</ImportantNote>
