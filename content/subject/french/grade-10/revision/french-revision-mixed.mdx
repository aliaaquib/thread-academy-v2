## French Revision: Mixed Practice

Revision isn’t re-reading — it’s retrieving. This lesson fires the essentials back at you so they stick.

<Definition term="Retrieval practice">
  **Retrieval practice** means recalling information from memory rather than re-reading it. Each successful recall strengthens the memory — which is why this lesson asks before it tells.
</Definition>

## Key points

- Greetings: bonjour, bonsoir, salut — and when each fits.
- Verbs: être, avoir, -er verbs in the present.
- Nouns: gender matters — le/la with every noun.
- Numbers to 100 and telling the time.

<Example title="Putting it together">
  — Bonjour! Je m’appelle Léa. Et toi?
— Salut Léa! Je m’appelle Hugo. Tu as quel âge?
— J’ai quatorze ans.
</Example>
