## Key ideas

### Reading authentic texts

Read for gist first and tolerate ambiguity. Note recurring vocabulary in a log. French literary tenses — the passé simple (il marcha) for narrative events and the imparfait for background — transform comprehension once recognised.

### Writing about film

Use technical terms: la mise en scène, le montage, la photographie. Structure essays with a thesis on the director's vision, analysis of key sequences, and a conclusion on the message. Context matters — the Nouvelle Vague reacted against studio convention.

**Key term — le plan:** A film shot — le gros plan (close-up), la bande-son (soundtrack), le montage (editing).

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
