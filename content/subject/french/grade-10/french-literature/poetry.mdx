## Key ideas

### Analysing poetry

Comment on versification (alexandrins — 12-syllable lines), rimes, rythme and images. Link technique to effect: Baudelaire's synaesthesia in 'Correspondances' fuses the senses to suggest a hidden unity — always connect device to meaning.

### Reading authentic texts

Read for gist first and tolerate ambiguity. Note recurring vocabulary in a log. French literary tenses — the passé simple (il marcha) for narrative events and the imparfait for background — transform comprehension once recognised.

**Key term — l'intrigue:** The plot — le dénouement (the resolution), le point culminant (the climax).

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Summarising instead of analysing** Marks come from explaining how meaning is created, not from retelling the story.
  - **Quoting without analysis** Each quotation needs a comment on technique and effect.
</ImportantNote>
