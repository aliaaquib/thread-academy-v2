## Key ideas

### Festivals through the year

Karneval (carnival) is huge in Köln and the Rhineland in February, with costumes and parades. The Oktoberfest in München actually begins in September and is the world's largest folk festival. In December, Weihnachtsmärkte (Christmas markets) fill town squares with Glühwein and Lebkuchen. Der Tag der Deutschen Einheit on 3rd October is Germany's national day.

### Daily life and mealtimes

German daily routine has its own vocabulary: der Alltag (everyday life), der Feierabend (time after work). The traditional evening meal is das Abendbrot, literally evening bread — cold cuts, cheese and bread. Shops often close on Sundays (der Sonntag ist Ruhetag), so Samstag is the big shopping day. Ich kaufe am Samstag ein uses a separable verb: einkaufen → ich kaufe ein.

**Key term — Die DACH-Länder:** Deutschland, Österreich and die Schweiz — the three main German-speaking countries, named after their car registration codes D, A and CH.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: German Cities">
  Name the capitals of the three DACH countries in German.

  Die Hauptstädte sind Berlin, Wien und Bern.

  **Answer:** Die Hauptstädte sind Berlin, Wien und Bern.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Thinking German is only spoken in Germany** German is an official language in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Liechtenstein, with distinct national varieties.
  - **Assuming the Oktoberfest is in October** It traditionally starts in September in München — the name is historical.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Write a German sentence saying when the Tag der Deutschen Einheit is." hint="Use am + date, then verb second.">
    Am 3. Oktober ist der Tag der Deutschen Einheit.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="In which month would you visit a Weihnachtsmarkt, and say so in German." hint="Weihnachten = Christmas.">
    Der Weihnachtsmarkt findet im Dezember statt.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Give one way written German in Switzerland differs from German in Germany." hint="Swiss German avoids one special letter.">
    In der Schweiz schreibt man ss statt ß, zum Beispiel die Strasse.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Why is it surprising that the Oktoberfest is called Oktoberfest?" hint="Think about when it actually starts.">
    Weil es im September beginnt. / Because it begins in September.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
