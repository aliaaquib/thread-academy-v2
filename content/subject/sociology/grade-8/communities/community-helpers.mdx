## Key ideas

### Helpers have special roles

Doctors, nurses, firefighters, teachers, shopkeepers, bus drivers and bin collectors each do jobs the whole community depends on. Many helpers also volunteer on top of their paid work. Recognising these roles helps us understand that society only works when thousands of people do their part.

### A community is people, not just buildings

Streets and shops matter, but a community is really made of relationships: people who greet each other, help in trouble and celebrate together. Sociologists say communities give us a sense of belonging beyond our families. A place full of strangers is just a location; a place full of neighbours is a community.

**Key term — Community:** A group of people who live in the same place or share interests, and who look out for each other.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Community Helpers">
  Name three community helpers and say what each one does.

  Any three with correct jobs, e.g. firefighter, puts out fires; teacher, educates children; shopkeeper, provides food and goods.

  **Answer:** Any three with correct jobs, e.g. firefighter, puts out fires; teacher, educates children; shopkeeper, provides food and goods.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Believing only uniformed workers are helpers** Shopkeepers, volunteers, neighbours and even children who tidy up are community helpers too.
  - **Thinking a community is only the place where you live** You can also belong to a school community, a club community or an online community of shared interests.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="What is a community?" hint="It is about people, not buildings.">
    A group of people who live in the same place or share interests and look out for each other.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="A new family moves into your street and speaks little English. Give two ways to welcome them into the community." hint="Think about belonging.">
    Any two kind actions, e.g. say hello and introduce yourself, invite their children to play, show them where the shops and park are.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="You want to help your community this weekend. Suggest one realistic action and explain who it helps." hint="Small actions count.">
    Any realistic action with a clear beneficiary, e.g. litter-picking in the park helps everyone who uses it; visiting an elderly neighbour helps them feel less lonely.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is a volunteer?" hint="The key word is freely.">
    Someone who gives their time freely to help others without being paid.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
