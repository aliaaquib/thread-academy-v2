<Definition term="Telling the Time">
  Say the time with 点， 分， 半 and 刻， and ask 现在几点.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Telling the time

Hours use 点 (diǎn): 三点 (sān diǎn) is three o'clock. Minutes use 分 (fēn): 三点十分 (sān diǎn shí fēn) is 3:10. Half past is 半 (bàn): 三点半 (sān diǎn bàn). A quarter is 刻 (kè): 三点一刻 (sān diǎn yí kè) is 3:15, and 差一刻四点 (chà yí kè sì diǎn) is quarter to four. Ask the time with 现在几点？.

### Dates and birthdays

Dates run biggest to smallest: year 年， month 月， day 日 or 号. 2026年9月21日 is read èr líng èr liù nián jiǔ yuè èrshíyī rì, with the year spoken digit by digit. Birthday is 生日 (shēngrì): 我的生日是五月三号 (wǒ de shēngrì shì wǔ yuè sān hào) means my birthday is 3 May.

**Key term — 现在 (xiànzài):** Now. 现在几点？ (xiànzài jǐ diǎn?) asks what time is it now — use 几 (jǐ) for times, never 多少.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Telling the Time">
  What does 差一刻四点 mean as a time?

  Quarter to four — 3:45.

  **Answer:** Quarter to four — 3:45.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Asking the time with 多少** Time always uses 几: 现在几点？ is right, and 现在多少点？ is wrong.
  - **Writing dates month-first like English** Chinese order is year, month, day: 2026年9月21日 is right, never 9月21日2026年.
</ImportantNote>
