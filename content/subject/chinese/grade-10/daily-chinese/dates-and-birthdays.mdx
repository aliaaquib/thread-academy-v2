## Key ideas

### Dates and birthdays

Dates run biggest to smallest: year 年， month 月， day 日 or 号. 2026年9月21日 is read èr líng èr liù nián jiǔ yuè èrshíyī rì, with the year spoken digit by digit. Birthday is 生日 (shēngrì): 我的生日是五月三号 (wǒ de shēngrì shì wǔ yuè sān hào) means my birthday is 3 May.

### Telling the time

Hours use 点 (diǎn): 三点 (sān diǎn) is three o'clock. Minutes use 分 (fēn): 三点十分 (sān diǎn shí fēn) is 3:10. Half past is 半 (bàn): 三点半 (sān diǎn bàn). A quarter is 刻 (kè): 三点一刻 (sān diǎn yí kè) is 3:15, and 差一刻四点 (chà yí kè sì diǎn) is quarter to four. Ask the time with 现在几点？.

**Key term — 现在 (xiànzài):** Now. 现在几点？ (xiànzài jǐ diǎn?) asks what time is it now — use 几 (jǐ) for times, never 多少.

<WorkedExample title="Buy a shirt and bargain">
  Build a short shop dialogue: ask the price of a shirt, say it is too dear, and ask for it cheaper.

  Point and ask the price with 件, the measure word for clothes: 这件衬衫多少钱？ (zhè jiàn chènshān duōshao qián?).
  React: 太贵了！(tài guì le!, too expensive!).
  Bargain politely: 能便宜一点吗？ (néng piányi yìdiǎn ma?, can it be a bit cheaper?).

  **Answer:** A: 这件衬衫多少钱？ B: 八十块。 A: 太贵了！能便宜一点吗？ — (Zhè jiàn chènshān duōshao qián? Bāshí kuài. Tài guì le! Néng piányi yìdiǎn ma?) — How much is this shirt? Eighty yuan. Too dear! Can it be a bit cheaper?
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Writing dates month-first like English** Chinese order is year, month, day: 2026年9月21日 is right, never 9月21日2026年.
  - **Asking the time with 多少** Time always uses 几: 现在几点？ is right, and 现在多少点？ is wrong.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="How do you say 3:30 in Chinese?" hint="三点 plus 半.">
    三点半 (sān diǎn bàn).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What does 差一刻四点 mean as a time?" hint="Lacking a quarter, four o'clock.">
    Quarter to four — 3:45.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Read this date aloud: 2026年9月21日." hint="Year digit by digit.">
    èr líng èr liù nián jiǔ yuè èrshíyī rì — 21 September 2026.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Ask where the metro station is." hint="地铁站 plus 在哪儿？">
    请问，地铁站在哪儿？(Qǐngwèn, dìtiě zhàn zài nǎr?).
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
