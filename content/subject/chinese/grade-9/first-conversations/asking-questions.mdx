This lesson focuses on Asking Questions: use 吗， question words and 呢 to keep a conversation going.

<Definition term="Asking Questions">
  Use 吗， question words and 呢 to keep a conversation going.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Yes-no questions with 吗

Add 吗 to the end of any statement: 他是中国人吗？ (tā shì zhōngguó rén ma?, is he Chinese?). Answer yes by repeating the verb — 是 (shì) for 是 questions — or with the adjective: 好吗？ is answered 好 or 不好. There is no single word for yes.

### Question words stay in place

Unlike English, Chinese question words sit where the answer would go: 你叫什么名字？ (nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?, what is your name?), 你是哪国人？ (nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?, which country are you from?), 你家有几口人？ (nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?, how many people are in your family?). Key words: 谁 (shéi, who), 什么 (shénme, what), 哪儿 (nǎr, where), 几 (jǐ, how many, for small numbers).

**Key term — 吗 (ma):** The yes-no question particle. Add it to a statement to turn it into a question: 你是学生吗？ (nǐ shì xuéshēng ma?, are you a student?).

<WorkedExample title="Hold a mini conversation">
  Complete this dialogue: A greets B, asks B's name, asks if B is a student, and they say goodbye.

  A opens: 你好！你叫什么名字？ (Nǐ hǎo! Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?).
  B answers and returns the question with 呢: 我叫王芳。你呢？ (Wǒ jiào Wáng Fāng. Nǐ ne?).
  A gives their name and asks a 吗 question: 我叫李明。你是学生吗？ (Wǒ jiào Lǐ Míng. Nǐ shì xuéshēng ma?).
  B answers by repeating 是, and they part: 是，我是学生。再见！ (Shì, wǒ shì xuéshēng. Zàijiàn!).

  **Answer:** A: 你好！你叫什么名字？ B: 我叫王芳。你呢？ A: 我叫李明。你是学生吗？ B: 是，我是学生。再见！ — Hello! What's your name? I'm Wang Fang. And you? I'm Li Ming. Are you a student? Yes, I'm a student. Goodbye!
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Moving the question word to the front** Keep it in place: 你叫什么名字？ is right, while 什么你叫名字？ is wrong.
  - **Saying 你好 in every turn** 你好 opens a conversation but is not repeated like English hi. Use 呢 and question words to continue instead.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Turn You are English into a question." hint="Add 吗.">
    你是英国人吗？(Nǐ shì yīngguó rén ma?).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="How do you ask and you? after saying you're fine?" hint="Use 呢.">
    我很好，你呢？(Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Ask where someone lives." hint="哪儿.">
    你住在哪儿？(Nǐ zhù zài nǎr?).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Invite a friend to play football tomorrow." hint="明天 plus 一起 plus 怎么样.">
    明天我们一起踢足球，怎么样？(Míngtiān wǒmen yìqǐ tī zúqiú, zěnmeyàng?).
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
