Your world is family, friends, school and weekends — so this chapter teaches you to describe exactly that in Chinese. You will count your household, introduce your friends, name your subjects and explain what you love doing when school finishes.

This lesson focuses on My Family: name family members and say how many people are in your household.

<Definition term="My Family">
  Name family members and say how many people are in your household.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Talking about family with 有

Use 有 (yǒu, to have) for family: 我家有四口人 (wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén) means my family has four people, where 口 (kǒu) is the measure word for household members. List them with the enumeration comma 、: 爸爸、妈妈、姐姐和我.

### Describing people

Chinese links adjectives with 很 (hěn), which softens rather than intensifies: 我妈妈很漂亮 (wǒ māma hěn piàoliang) means my mum is pretty. 的 (de) shows possession: 我的朋友 (wǒ de péngyou) is my friend. For close family, 的 is usually dropped: say 我妈妈, not 我的妈妈.

**Key term — 爸爸 (bàba):** Dad. Family words usually repeat the syllable: 爸爸， 妈妈 (māma, mum), 哥哥 (gēge, elder brother), 姐姐 (jiějiě, elder sister).

<WorkedExample title="Describe your family">
  Say in Chinese: My family has four people: dad, mum, elder sister and me.

  Start with 我家有 (wǒ jiā yǒu, my family has) and the number with 口: 四口人.
  List the members with 、 and join the last one with 和 (hé, and): 爸爸、妈妈、姐姐和我.
  Put it together and check the tones.

  **Answer:** 我家有四口人：爸爸、妈妈、姐姐和我。(Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén: bàba, māma, jiějiě hé wǒ.) — My family has four people: dad, mum, elder sister and me.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying 我的妈妈 like English my mum** Drop 的 with close family: 我妈妈 sounds natural, while 我的妈妈 sounds distant.
  - **Using 和 to join verbs** 和 (hé) joins nouns only: 我和朋友去学校 is fine, but never use 和 where English would put and between two actions.
</ImportantNote>

  <PracticeItem question="What is the measure word in 四口人, and what is it for?" hint="口.">

  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name three school subjects in Chinese." hint="Maths, English, Chinese.">

    姐姐 (jiějiě).
  </PracticeItem>
