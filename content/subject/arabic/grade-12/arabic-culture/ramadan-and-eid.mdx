## Key ideas

### The year turns on Ramadan

During Ramadan, Muslims fast from the dawn prayer (الفجر) to the sunset prayer (المغرب), then break the fast with إفطار (ifṭār), traditionally beginning with dates. Nights come alive with family gatherings and the تراويح (tarāwīḥ) prayers. The month ends with عيد الفطر, and about two months later comes عيد الأضحى (ʿĪd al-Aḍḥā), the Festival of Sacrifice coinciding with the Hajj pilgrimage. Wish people رمضان كريم (Ramaḍān karīm, generous Ramadan) during the month and عيد مبارك (ʿĪd mubārak, blessed Eid) at the festivals — never the reverse.

### One language, many voices

The Arab world spans the 22 member states of the Arab League, from Morocco to Oman, and its Arabic is diglossic: Modern Standard Arabic rules news, education and formal writing, while everyday life runs on regional dialects — Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf and Maghrebi among them. Dialects can differ so much that speakers from opposite ends of the region may switch to MSA to understand each other. Learning MSA gives you the key to all of them; learning a dialect gives you a home in one.

**Key term — رمضان (Ramaḍān) — Ramadan:** The ninth month of the Islamic calendar, when Muslims fast from dawn to sunset — no food, drink or smoking in daylight hours.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Ramadan and Eid">
  During Ramadan, between which two daily markers do Muslims fast?

  From dawn (الفجر, al-fajr) to sunset (المغرب, al-maghrib).

  **Answer:** From dawn (الفجر, al-fajr) to sunset (المغرب, al-maghrib).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying رمضان كريم on Eid day** رمضان كريم belongs to Ramadan itself. On Eid, say عيد مبارك.
  - **Offering or taking with the left hand** Always use the right hand for giving, receiving and eating — the left hand is considered unclean in Arab etiquette.
</ImportantNote>

  <PracticeItem question="What is the difference between MSA and a dialect?" hint="Think newsreader versus home.">

  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name two styles of Arabic calligraphy." hint="Naskh is one; thuluth and kūfī are others.">
