## Key ideas

### Trade can be unfair

In ordinary trade, powerful buyers can force down the prices paid to small farmers, leaving them in poverty. Fair trade certification sets a minimum price and extra payments for community projects, so that farmers and workers get a steadier, fairer income. Choosing fair trade products is one way consumers can support fairer trade.

### No country makes everything

Countries cannot produce everything they need — the climate may not suit, or the resources may not exist. So they buy (import) what they lack and sell (export) what they produce well. This is why Britain imports bananas but exports cars and financial services.

**Key term — Trade:** The exchange of goods and services between people, businesses or countries. International trade is trade across national borders.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Trade Routes">
  Name two ways goods are transported in international trade.

  By sea (container ships) and by air (cargo planes); land routes by lorry and rail also count.

  **Answer:** By sea (container ships) and by air (cargo planes); land routes by lorry and rail also count.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Thinking trade only means physical goods** Services are traded too — banking, tourism and software are major exports for many countries. Trade is not just ships full of boxes.
  - **Assuming the cheapest price is always the best deal** Very low prices can mean farmers or workers were paid too little. Fair trade shows that who gets paid fairly matters as well as what you pay.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="What is the aim of fair trade?" hint="It is about fairer prices for producers.">
    Fair trade aims to give farmers and workers in poorer countries a fairer price, better working conditions and extra payments for community projects.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is an import?" hint="It comes INTO a country.">
    An import is a good or service bought from another country.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is an export?" hint="It goes OUT of a country.">
    An export is a good or service sold to another country.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="If France sells cheese to Japan, is the cheese an import or an export for France?" hint="It is leaving France.">
    It is an export for France (and an import for Japan).
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
