## Time and Daily Life

Japanese time is regular: hours with 時 (ji), minutes with 分 (fun/pun), and days that stack neatly.

<Definition term="Ji / fun">
  **時 (ji)** marks hours: 三時 (san-ji) = 3 o’clock. **分** marks minutes with sound changes: 三分 (san-pun), 六分 (roppun) — the pun/fun alternation is the classic beginner hurdle.
</Definition>

## Key points

- 今何時ですか? (ima nan-ji desu ka?) = what time is it?
- 半 (han) = half: 三時半 = 3:30.
- Days: 今日 (kyō) today, 明日 (ashita) tomorrow, 昨日 (kinō) yesterday.
- Routine verbs: 起きます (okimasu = wake up), 食べます (tabemasu = eat), 寝ます (nemasu = sleep).

<Example title="Daily routine">
  毎日七時に起きます。朝ご飯を食べます。
(Mainichi shichi-ji ni okimasu. Asagohan o tabemasu. = I wake at 7 every day. I eat breakfast.)
</Example>
