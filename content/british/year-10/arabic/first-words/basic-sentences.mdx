## Basic Sentences

‘Ana tilmeedh’ — literally ‘I student’ — means ‘I am a student’. Present-tense ‘is’ simply doesn’t appear.

<Definition term="Nominal sentence">
  A **nominal sentence** (جملة اسمية) starts with a noun/pronoun and needs no verb for ‘is’: الكتاب جديد (al-kitab jadeed) = ‘the book [is] new’.
</Definition>

## Key points

- Pronouns: أنا (ana = I), أنت (anta/anti = you m/f), هو (huwa = he), هي (hiya = she).
- No present-tense ‘is/am/are’ — just put subject and description together.
- Adjectives follow nouns and agree in gender: كتاب جديد (new book, m), مدرسة جديدة (new school, f).
- Questions: هل (hal) at the start turns a statement into a yes/no question.

<Example title="Describing things">
  البيت كبير (al-bayt kabeer) = the house [is] big.
هل البيت كبير؟ = is the house big?
</Example>
