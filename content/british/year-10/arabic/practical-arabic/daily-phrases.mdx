## Daily Phrases

A handful of phrases — used constantly — carry most daily conversation. Master these and you’ll navigate markets, taxis and small talk.

<Definition term="Inshallah">
  **Inshallah** (إن شاء الله) = ‘God willing’ — attached to future plans. It’s both piety and pragmatism: acknowledging the future isn’t fully ours to promise.
</Definition>

## Key points

- كم السعر؟ (kam as-si‘r?) = how much? — bargaining’s opening move.
- غالي! (ghali!) = expensive! — said with a smile, it’s negotiation, not insult.
- ممتاز (mumtaz) = excellent. طيب (tayyib) = good/okay.
- Time: اليوم (today), غداً (tomorrow), الأن (now).

<Example title="Bargaining">
  — كم السعر؟
— عشرون درهماً.
— غالي! خمسة؟
— لا، ثمانية… طيب، اتفقنا! (Deal!)
</Example>
