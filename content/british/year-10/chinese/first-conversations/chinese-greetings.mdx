## Greetings

Chinese greetings show care through questions: ‘Have you eaten?’ (你吃了吗) is warmth, not nosiness.

<Definition term="Ni hao">
  **你好 (nǐ hǎo)** = ‘you good’ — the universal hello. **您好 (nín hǎo)** is the respectful form for elders and formal contexts.
</Definition>

## Key points

- 你好! — hello. 再见 (zàijiàn) — goodbye (‘again see’).
- 谢谢 (xièxie) = thank you. 不客气 (búkèqì) = you’re welcome.
- 我叫… (wǒ jiào…) = I’m called… — introducing yourself.
- 你吃了吗? (nǐ chī le ma?) = ‘have you eaten?’ — answer 吃了 (eaten!) warmly.

<Example title="Meeting someone">
  — 你好! 我叫大卫。你叫什么名字?
— 你好，大卫! 我叫丽丽。很高兴认识你!
(Hello! I’m called David. What’s your name? — Hello, David! I’m Lili. Nice to meet you!)
</Example>

<Summary>
  - 你好 hellos; 您好 respects.
  - 我叫… introduces you.
  - 谢谢/不客气 handle thanks.
  - Food questions are affection.
</Summary>
