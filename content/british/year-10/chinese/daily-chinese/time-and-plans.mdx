## Time and Plans

Chinese time is systematic: weeks are numbered (星期一 = week-one = Monday), and time phrases stack neatly.

<Definition term="Xingqi">
  **星期 (xīngqī)** = week. Days are numbered: 星期一 Monday … 星期五 Friday, 星期六 Saturday, 星期天/星期日 Sunday.
</Definition>

## Key points

- Time: 点 (diǎn) = o’clock: 三点 = 3 o’clock; 分 = minutes; 半 = half.
- 现在几点? (xiànzài jǐ diǎn?) = what time is it now?
- Days: 今天 today, 明天 tomorrow, 昨天 yesterday.
- Plans: 明天三点见! (See you tomorrow at 3!)

<Example title="Making a plan">
  — 明天你有空吗?
— 有空。几点?
— 下午三点半。
— 好，明天见!
(Are you free tomorrow? — Yes. What time? — 3:30pm. — Great, see you!)
</Example>

<Summary>
  - 点 = o’clock; 半 = half.
  - Weekdays are numbered: 星期一 = Monday.
  - 今天/明天/昨天 = today/tomorrow/yesterday.
  - Time words stack neatly.
</Summary>
