## Key ideas

### A diverse Spanish-speaking world

Spain has strong regional identities, with Catalan, Basque and Galician languages and cultures; Latin America spans nineteen countries with distinct slang, food and music. Contemporary debates include youth unemployment, gender equality and the legacy of Franco.

### Fiestas and identity

Festivals blend Catholic, indigenous and local traditions: Las Fallas in Valencia, Semana Santa processions, Día de Muertos in Mexico. They are living expressions of regional and national identity — and major tourist draws.

**Key term — la sobremesa:** The leisurely chat around the table after a meal — a cherished social ritual in Spain and Latin America.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Regional Diversity">
  Name two regional languages of Spain besides Castilian.

  Catalan and Basque (also Galician).

  **Answer:** Catalan and Basque (also Galician).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Generalising about 'all Spaniards'** Spain and Latin America are hugely diverse — qualify claims by region.
  - **Ignoring indigenous cultures** Latin American identity includes rich indigenous heritage — mention it, not just Spanish colonial influence.
</ImportantNote>
