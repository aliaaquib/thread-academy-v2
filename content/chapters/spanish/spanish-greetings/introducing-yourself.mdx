## Key ideas

### Introducing yourself

Me llamo… states your name, and Tengo… años gives your age — note Spanish uses 'I have' for age: Tengo diez años (I am ten). Soy de… says where you are from: Soy de Londres (I am from London).

### Politeness opens doors

Por favor (please), gracias (thank you), perdón (sorry / excuse me) and de nada (you're welcome) cost nothing and earn goodwill. Spanish speakers use tú with friends and usted with strangers or elders — with unfamiliar adults, usted is the safer choice.

**Key term — hola:** Hello — the universal greeting, friendly at any time of day and in any Spanish-speaking country.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Introducing Yourself">
  How do you say 'hello' in Spanish?

  ¡Hola!

  **Answer:** ¡Hola!
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying 'yo soy diez años'** Age uses tener, not ser: Tengo diez años (I have ten years).
  - **Using 'buenas noches' only as goodbye** It also means 'good evening' as a greeting when you arrive somewhere at night.
</ImportantNote>
