Numbers and colours are among the most useful words you will ever learn: they let you shop, tell the time, give your age and describe the world. This chapter builds confident counting to 100 and the essential colour words, including how they agree with nouns.

This lesson focuses on Numbers to Twenty: count from uno to veinte.

<Definition term="Numbers to Twenty">
  Count from uno to veinte.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Counting to 100

Learn 1–15 by heart, then spot the patterns: 16–19 are dieci + number (dieciséis), the tens are veinte, treinta, cuarenta… noventa, and you join with y: treinta y dos (32). Cien is 100. Practise with ages, prices and phone numbers.

### Word order

Unlike English, colours usually come after the noun: un perro negro (a black dog). The natural rhythm is number + noun + colour: tres gatos blancos (three white cats).

**Key term — quince:** Fifteen. Numbers 16–19 fuse into a single word: dieciséis, diecisiete, dieciocho, diecinueve.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Numbers to Twenty">
  How do you say 21 in Spanish?

  Veintiuno.

  **Answer:** Veintiuno.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Forgetting the accent in dieciséis** 16–19 carry written accents: dieciséis, veintidós, veintitrés, veintiséis.
  - **Saying 'uno libro'** Uno shortens to un before masculine nouns: un libro.
</ImportantNote>
