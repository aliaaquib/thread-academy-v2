## Key ideas

### Ceremonies show respect

When people stand for an anthem or observe a silence, they show respect for shared memories and values. Taking part respectfully, even when the ceremony is not from your own background, is a sign of good manners and kindness.

### Symbols carry big meanings

A symbol is something small that stands for something much bigger. A heart shape can stand for love, and a flag can stand for a whole country. Because symbols are easy to recognise, they help people feel connected to shared ideas.

**Key term — respect:** Treating people, symbols and ceremonies with care and good manners. Showing respect means behaving thoughtfully even when something is new to you.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Showing Respect">
  How should you behave during a flag ceremony?

  Stand still, stay quiet and listen. This shows respect for the people and memories the ceremony honours.

  **Answer:** Stand still, stay quiet and listen. This shows respect for the people and memories the ceremony honours.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Forgetting to be respectful** Talking, laughing or fidgeting during a ceremony can upset people for whom it is meaningful. Standing quietly and listening shows respect even if you are still learning. Good behaviour during ceremonies matters.
  - **Thinking a flag is just a pretty picture** Every part of a flag is chosen for a reason. Colours and shapes often tell a story about a country's history, land or beliefs. Looking closely at a flag can teach you something new.
</ImportantNote>
