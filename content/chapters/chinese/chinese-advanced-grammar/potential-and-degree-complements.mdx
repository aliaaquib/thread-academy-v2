## Key ideas

### Result, potential and degree complements

Result complements state the outcome: 听懂 (tīngdǒng, hear and understand), 看见 (kànjiàn, catch sight of), 找到 (zhǎodào, find). Insert 得 or 不 for potential: 听得懂 (tīng de dǒng, can understand by hearing) versus 听不懂 (tīng bu dǒng, cannot). For degree, use verb plus 得 plus description: 他汉语说得很好 (tā hànyǔ shuō de hěn hǎo, he speaks Chinese very well). Do not confuse complement 得 with possessive 的 or adverbial 地 — same sound, three jobs.

### The 把 construction

Pattern: subject plus 把 plus object plus verb plus result or direction complement, often with 了. 我把门关上了 (wǒ bǎ mén guānshang le, I closed the door). 把 moves the object before the verb to stress what was done to it — but the verb cannot stand alone: it needs a complement such as 完 or 上， or a 了 marking a completed result. 我把作业做 is wrong; 我把作业做完了 is right.

**Key term — 把 (bǎ):** The disposal marker. Subject plus 把 plus object plus verb plus complement: 我把作业做完了 (wǒ bǎ zuòyè zuò wán le, I finished the homework).

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Potential and Degree Complements">
  Say I can understand (by hearing) with a potential complement.

  我听得懂。(Wǒ tīng de dǒng.).

  **Answer:** 我听得懂。(Wǒ tīng de dǒng.).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Mixing up 的， 地 and 得** 的 marks possession (我的书), 地 marks adverbs (慢慢地走), 得 marks complements (跑得快). Same sound, three jobs.
  - **Using 把 with a bare verb** 我把门关 is wrong. The verb needs a complement or result: 我把门关上了.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Say she writes characters beautifully with a degree complement." hint="写得 plus 很漂亮.">
    她汉字写得很漂亮。(Tā hànzì xiě de hěn piàoliang.).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is wrong with 我把衣服洗?" hint="Bare verb after 把.">
    The verb needs a complement: 我把衣服洗干净了 (I washed the clothes clean).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Rewrite with 把: 他喝完了茶." hint="The object is 茶.">
    他把茶喝完了。(Tā bǎ chá hē wán le.).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Translate: My phone was stolen." hint="手机 plus 被偷了.">
    我的手机被偷了。(Wǒ de shǒujī bèi tōu le.).
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
