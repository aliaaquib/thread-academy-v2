<Definition term="Describe Things">

</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Colours and describing nouns

Six key colours: 红色 hóngsè red, 黄色 huángsè yellow, 蓝色 lánsè blue, 绿色 lǜsè green, 黑色 hēisè black, 白色 báisè white. Put the colour before the noun: 红苹果 (hóng píngguǒ) is a red apple, and 蓝书包 (lán shūbāo) is a blue schoolbag.

### Counting to ten

Learn 一 yī, 二 èr, 三 sān, 四 sì, 五 wǔ, 六 liù, 七 qī, 八 bā, 九 jiǔ, 十 shí with their tones. Chinese children often count to ten on one hand using finger gestures — watch and copy them, as it makes the numbers stick.

**Key term — 个 (gè):** The everyday measure word. Chinese counts three apples as 三个苹果 (sān gè píngguǒ): number, measure word, noun.

<WorkedExample title="Say five red apples">
  How do you say five red apples in Chinese?

  Take the number: five is 五 (wǔ).
  Add the measure word 个 (gè): 五个.
  Add the colour before the noun: 红 (hóng) plus 苹果 (píngguǒ, apple).

  **Answer:** 五个红苹果 (wǔ gè hóng píngguǒ) — five red apples. The order is always number, measure word, colour, noun.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying 二个苹果 for two apples** Two before a measure word is 两 (liǎng), not 二: 两个苹果 (liǎng gè píngguǒ).
  - **Putting the colour after the noun** Chinese keeps the colour first, just as English does: 红苹果 is right, and 苹果红 is wrong.
</ImportantNote>
