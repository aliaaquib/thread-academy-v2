## Key ideas

### School and free time

Core subjects: 数学 (shùxué, maths), 英语 (yīngyǔ, English), 汉语 (hànyǔ, Chinese), 科学 (kēxué, science). Say what you like with 喜欢 (xǐhuan): 我喜欢数学 (wǒ xǐhuan shùxué). Hobbies pair action verbs with nouns: 踢足球 (tī zúqiú, play football), 听音乐 (tīng yìnyuè, listen to music), 看书 (kàn shū, read books).

### Talking about family with 有

Use 有 (yǒu, to have) for family: 我家有四口人 (wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén) means my family has four people, where 口 (kǒu) is the measure word for household members. List them with the enumeration comma 、: 爸爸、妈妈、姐姐和我.

**Key term — 学校 (xuéxiào):** School. Combine it with adjectives: 学校很大 (xuéxiào hěn dà) means the school is very big.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: School Subjects">
  Name three school subjects in Chinese.

  数学 (shùxué), 英语 (yīngyǔ), 汉语 (hànyǔ).

  **Answer:** 数学 (shùxué), 英语 (yīngyǔ), 汉语 (hànyǔ).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Forgetting 很 before the adjective** Say 我妈妈很高兴, not 我妈妈高兴 — a bare adjective after the subject sounds unfinished in Chinese.
  - **Saying 我的妈妈 like English my mum** Drop 的 with close family: 我妈妈 sounds natural, while 我的妈妈 sounds distant.
</ImportantNote>
