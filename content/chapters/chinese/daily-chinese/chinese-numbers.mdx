## Numbers

Once you know 1–10, you can count to 99 by combination. Chinese children learn arithmetic faster — researchers blame the logical number words.

<Definition term="Logical numbers">
  Chinese builds numbers transparently: 11 = 十一 (ten-one), 20 = 二十 (two-ten), 25 = 二十五 (two-ten-five). No ‘eleven’ or ‘twelve’ irregulars.
</Definition>

## Key points

- 1–10: 一 二 三 四 五 六 七 八 九 十.
- 11 = 十一, 20 = 二十, 99 = 九十九.
- 100 = 一百, 1000 = 一千, 10,000 = 一万 (wan — Chinese counts in ten-thousands).
- Money: 块 (kuài) = yuan colloquially: 三块五 = 3.5 yuan.

<Example title="At the market">
  — 这个多少钱?
— 二十五块。
— 太贵了! 二十块怎么样?
(How much? — 25 yuan. — Too expensive! How about 20?)
</Example>

<Summary>
  - Numbers combine logically: ten-one, two-ten.
  - 1–10 unlocks 1–99.
  - 万 = 10,000 (group by ten-thousands).
  - 块 = yuan in speech.
</Summary>
