The Earth has seven huge land masses called continents and five vast oceans between them. In this chapter you will learn their names, find them on a world map, and use compass directions to describe where they are.

This lesson focuses on The Seven Continents: name all seven continents and locate each one on a world map.

<Definition term="The Seven Continents">

</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Seven continents of very different sizes

Asia is the largest continent and Australia the smallest. Europe and Asia are joined as one giant landmass, but we treat them as separate continents because of their different histories and cultures. Antarctica is covered in ice and is the coldest continent.

### Oceans cover most of the planet

About seven-tenths of the Earth's surface is ocean. The Pacific is the largest ocean — bigger than all the land put together. The Arctic Ocean around the North Pole is the smallest and coldest.

**Key term — continent:** A very large area of land — there are seven: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North America and South America.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: The Seven Continents">
  Name the seven continents.

  Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North America and South America.

  **Answer:** Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia, Europe, North America and South America.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Mixing up the Arctic and Antarctica** The Arctic is the ocean and ice around the North Pole; Antarctica is the icy continent around the South Pole. Their names sound alike but they are at opposite ends of the Earth.
  - **Thinking north is always 'up' in real life** North is at the top of most maps, but in the real world it is simply a direction you can face — a compass needle points towards it.
</ImportantNote>
