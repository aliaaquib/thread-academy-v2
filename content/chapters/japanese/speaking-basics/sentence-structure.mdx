## Sentence Structure

私は茶を飲みます (watashi wa ocha o nomimasu) — ‘I tea drink’. Verb last, particles marking roles: the core pattern of Japanese.

<Definition term="Particle">
  **Particles** are short words marking grammatical roles: は (wa) = topic, が (ga) = subject, を (o) = direct object, に (ni) = direction/time. They’re the skeleton of Japanese sentences.
</Definition>

## Key points

- Word order: subject–object–verb. The verb is always last.
- 私は学生です (watashi wa gakusei desu) = I am a student.
- Questions: add か (ka): 学生ですか? = are you a student?
- Negation: じゃありません — 学生じゃありません (I’m not a student).

<Example title="Building sentences">
  私は本を読みます。
(watashi wa hon o yomimasu = I read books — literally ‘I book read’.)
</Example>
