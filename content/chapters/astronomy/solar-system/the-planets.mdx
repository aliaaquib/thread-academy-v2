## The Planets

My Very Excellent Mother Just Served Us Noodles — Mercury to Neptune. Four small rocky worlds inside, four giants outside, divided by the asteroid belt.

<Definition term="Planet">
  A **planet** orbits a star, is round under its own gravity, and has cleared its orbit of other debris. That third rule demoted Pluto in 2006 — it shares its zone with other Kuiper Belt objects.
</Definition>

## Key points

- Terrestrial planets (Mercury–Mars): small, rocky, few or no moons.
- Gas giants (Jupiter, Saturn) and ice giants (Uranus, Neptune): huge, no solid surface.
- Jupiter is 1,300 times Earth’s volume — more than twice the mass of all other planets combined.
- Exoplanets: 5,000+ worlds found around other stars and counting.

<Example title="A day on Venus">
  Venus spins backwards, and so slowly that its day (243 Earth days) is longer than its year (225 Earth days). Sunrise to sunrise there takes 117 Earth days.
</Example>
