## Key ideas

### The solar system formed from a cloud

About 4.6 billion years ago, a giant cloud of gas and dust collapsed under gravity. Most of it became the Sun; the rest flattened into a disc where planets grew by clumping together. This is why all the planets orbit in the same direction and nearly the same plane.

### Small bodies matter

Asteroids, comets and dwarf planets are leftovers from the solar system's formation. Comets delivered water and organic material to the early Earth, and asteroid impacts have shaped life's history, including the extinction of the dinosaurs 66 million years ago.

**Key term — Light year:** The distance light travels in one year, about 9.5 trillion kilometres. Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth, but years to reach the nearest stars.

<WorkedExample title="How far is Mars in light-minutes?">
  Mars is about 228 million km from the Sun and Earth is about 150 million km. When Mars and Earth are on the same side of the Sun, roughly how long does sunlight take to reach Mars?

  Sunlight travels at about 300,000 km per second.
  Divide Mars's distance by the speed of light: 228,000,000 ÷ 300,000 = 760 seconds.
  Convert seconds to minutes: 760 ÷ 60 ≈ 12.7 minutes.
  Compare with Earth: light takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth, so Mars gets its sunlight about 5 minutes later.

  **Answer:** About 12 to 13 minutes for sunlight to reach Mars.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Mixing up a light year (distance) with a year (time)** A light year is a distance, the distance light travels in a year. Saying a star is '5 light years away' describes how far it is, not how long ago something happened.
  - **Thinking the asteroid belt is crowded like in films** Asteroids in the belt are typically millions of kilometres apart; spacecraft fly through it without dodging anything.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="What is one astronomical unit (AU)?" hint="It is defined by Earth's orbit.">
    The average distance from Earth to the Sun, about 150 million km.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Where is the asteroid belt, and what is it made of?" hint="It lies between two planets.">
    It orbits between Mars and Jupiter and is made of rocky fragments left over from the solar system's formation.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Why do all the planets orbit the Sun in the same direction?" hint="They formed from a spinning disc.">
    The planets formed from a rotating disc of gas and dust around the young Sun, so they inherited the disc's direction of rotation.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Light from the Sun takes 8 minutes to reach Earth. Roughly how long does it take to reach Jupiter, about 5.2 AU from the Sun?" hint="Multiply 8 minutes by 5.2.">
    About 42 minutes (5.2 × 8 = 41.6).
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
