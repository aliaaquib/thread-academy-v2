## Key ideas

### Introducing yourself in three sentences

A perfect first introduction needs just three sentences: your name (Ich heiße...), your age (Ich bin ... Jahre alt) and where you are from (Ich komme aus...). Each verb is in second position, and und can join two of them: Ich heiße Anna und ich bin elf Jahre alt.

### Du and Sie

German has two words for you: du for friends, family and children, and Sie for adults you do not know well and in formal situations. Use Wie geht es dir? with friends and Wie geht es Ihnen? formally. Getting this right is one of the most important social skills in German.

**Key term — Ich heiße ...:** My name is ..., literally I am called .... The verb heißen is the natural way to give your name.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Introducing Yourself">
  How do you say My name is ... in German?

  Ich heiße ... / Mein Name ist ..

  **Answer:** Ich heiße ... / Mein Name ist ..
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying Ich habe 11 Jahre** Age uses sein, not haben: say Ich bin elf Jahre alt (I am eleven years old).
  - **Using du with strangers** Use Sie with unfamiliar adults: Wie geht es Ihnen? Save du for friends, family and children.
</ImportantNote>
