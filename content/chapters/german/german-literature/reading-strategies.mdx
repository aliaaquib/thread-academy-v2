## Key ideas

### Reading strategies for real texts

Do not translate word by word. First skim for the Handlung (plot): who are the Hauptfiguren (main characters) and what is the Problem? Use cognates — German and English share many words, like das Haus (house) and der Garten (garden). Guess unknown words from context, then check only the key ones. Reading the same tale you already know in English makes your first German book much easier.

### The world of the Märchen

A Märchen is a fairy tale, and the Brothers Grimm collected the most famous German ones in their Kinder- und Hausmärchen, first published in 1812. Tales like Hänsel und Gretel, Rotkäppchen and Aschenputtel follow clear patterns: a hero in danger, magical help, and a happy ending. Typical phrases include Es war einmal... (Once upon a time...) and ...und sie lebten glücklich bis ans Ende ihrer Tage.

**Key term — Die Strophe:** A verse or stanza of a poem. German poetry often uses rhyme (der Reim) and rhythm to make language memorable.

<WorkedExample title="Retelling Rotkäppchen">
  Summarise the tale of Rotkäppchen (Little Red Riding Hood) in four simple German sentences: introduce her, say she walked through the forest to her grandmother, mention the wolf and the hunter, and give the happy ending.

  Step 1: Open with the classic Es war einmal ein Mädchen namens Rotkäppchen.
  Step 2: Use the simple past ging (went) for storytelling: Sie ging durch den Wald zu ihrer Großmutter.
  Step 3: Add the danger with wollte (wanted to): Ein Wolf wollte sie fressen, aber ein Jäger rettete sie.
  Step 4: Close with the traditional happy ending: Am Ende waren alle glücklich.

  **Answer:** Es war einmal ein Mädchen namens Rotkäppchen. Sie ging durch den Wald zu ihrer Großmutter. Ein Wolf wollte sie fressen, aber ein Jäger rettete sie. Am Ende waren alle glücklich.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Translating every single word** Skim for the plot first using cognates and context; only look up words that block your understanding of the story.
  - **Inventing quotes from authors** Never make up quotations. Describe plots and characters in your own German words instead.
</ImportantNote>
