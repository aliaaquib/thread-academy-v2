## Key ideas

### Death is not the end - but what comes next?

Most religions teach that something of us survives death. Christians and Muslims look forward to resurrection and judgement leading to heaven or hell. Hindus, Buddhists, Sikhs and Jains teach rebirth: the soul is reborn according to karma until it reaches liberation. These beliefs comfort the dying and encourage moral living.

### Different pictures of the divine

Monotheists, such as Jews, Christians, Muslims and Sikhs, believe in one God. Hindus speak of one ultimate reality, Brahman, expressed through many deities. Buddhists focus on enlightenment rather than a creator God, and humanists live without belief in God at all. These are not small differences of taste - they change how followers pray, worship and understand the world.

**Key term — soul:** The spiritual part of a person, which many religions believe survives death.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Good and Evil">
  What is karma?

  The teaching, found in Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism and Jainism, that good and bad actions have consequences, including in future lives.

  **Answer:** The teaching, found in Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism and Jainism, that good and bad actions have consequences, including in future lives.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Atheism means hating religion** Atheism is simply the belief that no God exists; many atheists live by strong moral codes and respect religious people.
  - **Hindus worship many completely separate gods** Many Hindus understand the deities as different expressions of one ultimate reality, Brahman.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Name one religion that teaches rebirth and one that teaches resurrection." hint="Rebirth: think Dharmic.">
    Rebirth: Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism or Jainism. Resurrection: Christianity or Islam.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is the difference between theism and atheism?" hint="One affirms, one denies.">
    Theism is belief that God or gods exist; atheism is belief that they do not.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What does an agnostic claim about God?" hint="It is about knowledge.">
    That we cannot know whether God exists.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="How might belief in karma affect someone's behaviour?" hint="Think before acting.">
    It encourages careful, kind behaviour, because harmful actions are believed to bring bad consequences.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
