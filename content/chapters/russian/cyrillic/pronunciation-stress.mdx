## Pronunciation and Stress

Москва is pronounced ‘maskVA’, not ‘moskva’. Unstressed vowels shrink — the single biggest key to sounding Russian.

<Definition term="Vowel reduction">
  **Vowel reduction**: unstressed о sounds like а (‘a’), unstressed е sounds like и (‘i’). Молоко (milk) is pronounced ‘malaKO’.
</Definition>

## Key points

- Stress can fall on any syllable — dictionaries mark it; learners must memorise it.
- ЗАмок (ZAmok) = castle; замОк (zaMOK) = lock. Stress alone distinguishes them.
- Consonants: р is rolled; ж like ‘zh’ (measure); ц like ‘ts’; щ like soft ‘shsh’.
- Ы is the famous vowel — between ‘i’ and ‘u’, tongue pulled back.

<Example title="Moloko">
  МОЛОКО → malaKO. Three о’s, one stressed — the unstressed two reduce to ‘a’. Say ‘moloko’ and Russians hear a foreigner instantly.
</Example>

## Practice

    ‘malako’ — unstressed о reduces to ‘a’; only the stressed о keeps its sound.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="How do замок and замок differ?" hint="They’re spelled identically.">
    Stress position: ZAmok (castle) vs zaMOK (lock).
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>

<Summary>
  - Unstressed о→a, е→i.
  - Stress is free — memorise it per word.
  - Stress can change meaning (zamok).
  - Ы, ж, щ need dedicated practice.
</Summary>
