## Key ideas

### Любить: loving things in the present tense

The verb любить (lyubít' — to love, to like) conjugates as я люблю (ya lyublyú — I love), ты любишь (ty lyúbish — you love), он/она любит (on/oná lyúbit — he/she loves). So Я люблю музыку (ya lyublyú múzyku — I love music), and Моя сестра любит спорт (moyá sestrá lyúbit sport — my sister loves sport).

### Мой, моя, моё: possessives agree

The word for 'my' changes to match the noun: мой брат (moy brat — my brother, masculine), моя сестра (moyá sestrá — my sister, feminine), моё хобби (moyó khóbbi — my hobby, neuter). Get the gender right and the possessive follows automatically.

**Key term — мама (máma) — mum:** The word for mother. Note the double М and the stress on the first syllable: МА-ма.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Hobbies and Free Time">
  Translate 'I love music' into Russian.

  Я люблю музыку (ya lyublyú múzyku).

  **Answer:** Я люблю музыку (ya lyublyú múzyku).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying моя брат** Брат (brat — brother) is masculine, so it must be мой брат (moy brat — my brother), not моя брат.
  - **Forgetting the prepositional ending in в школе** It is в школе (f shkolé — at school), not в школа. The -а changes to -е after в.
</ImportantNote>
