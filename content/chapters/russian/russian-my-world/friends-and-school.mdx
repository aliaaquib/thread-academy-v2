## Key ideas

### В школе: the prepositional case

To say where something happens, Russian uses the prepositional case after в (in): школа (shkóla — school) becomes в школе (f shkolé — at school). Feminine nouns ending in -а change -а to -е: Я учусь в школе (ya uchús' f shkolé — I study at school).

### Мой, моя, моё: possessives agree

The word for 'my' changes to match the noun: мой брат (moy brat — my brother, masculine), моя сестра (moyá sestrá — my sister, feminine), моё хобби (moyó khóbbi — my hobby, neuter). Get the gender right and the possessive follows automatically.

**Key term — друг (drug) — friend:** A masculine noun for a male friend; a female friend is подруга (podrúga). The plural друзья (druz'yá — friends) is irregular — worth memorising.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Friends and School">
  What is the plural of друг?

  Друзья (druz'yá — friends).

  **Answer:** Друзья (druz'yá — friends).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Forgetting the prepositional ending in в школе** It is в школе (f shkolé — at school), not в школа. The -а changes to -е after в.
  - **Using друг for a female friend** Друг (drug) is a male friend; a female friend is подруга (podrúga). Mixing them up will confuse your listener.
</ImportantNote>
