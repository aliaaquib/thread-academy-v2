Money is a tool: used well, it brings security and choices; used carelessly, it brings worry. This chapter builds healthy money habits early — earning, saving, spending wisely and budgeting.

This lesson focuses on Earning Money: discover the ways children and adults earn money.

<Definition term="Earning Money">
  Discover the ways children and adults earn money.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Pay yourself first

Most people spend first and save what is left — which is usually nothing. The golden habit is reversed: move savings aside the moment money arrives, then spend the rest guilt-free. Even £1 a week builds the habit and the pot.

### A simple budget

A child's budget has three jars: save, spend, share. Decide percentages — for example 40% save, 50% spend, 10% share — and split every pound that arrives. Tracking where money goes is the first step to controlling it.

**Key term — income:** Money coming in, such as pocket money, earnings from chores or wages from a job.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Earning Money">
  Name one way a child could earn money.

  Any one of: chores, dog walking, car washing, selling crafts, paper round.

  **Answer:** Any one of: chores, dog walking, car washing, selling crafts, paper round.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saving 'what is left'** There is rarely anything left — move savings first, the moment money arrives.
  - **Borrowing for wants** Borrowed money costs extra in interest — never borrow for things you merely want.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="What does 'pay yourself first' mean?" hint="Save before…">
    Move money into savings the moment income arrives, before spending anything.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Why is a budget useful?" hint="Think about control.">
    It plans where money goes, so you save enough, avoid overspending and stay in control.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is an impulse buy?" hint="Unplanned…">
    An unplanned purchase made on sudden desire, without thinking it through.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="You want £30 trainers and get £5 a week. If you save it all, how many weeks?" hint="Divide.">
    6 weeks (£30 ÷ £5).
  </PracticeItem>
