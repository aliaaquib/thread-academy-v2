## Greetings

‘As-salamu ‘alaykum’ — peace be upon you. The reply returns the peace and adds more. Greetings in Arabic are never rushed.

<Definition term="As-salamu alaykum">
  **As-salamu ‘alaykum** (السلام عليكم) = ‘peace be upon you’ — the universal greeting. Reply: **wa ‘alaykum as-salam** (وعليكم السلام), ‘and upon you be peace’.
</Definition>

## Key points

- صباح الخير (sabah al-khayr) = good morning; reply صباح النور.
- مساء الخير (masa’ al-khayr) = good evening.
- اسمي… (ismi…) = my name is…
- شكراً (shukran) = thank you; عفواً (afwan) = you’re welcome/excuse me.

<Example title="Meeting someone">
  — السلام عليكم!
— وعليكم السلام. اسمي ليلى. ما اسمك؟
— اسمي عمر. تشرفنا! (Pleased to meet you!)
</Example>

<Summary>
  - As-salamu alaykum = peace be upon you.
  - Greetings come in pairs — always reply.
  - Ismi… introduces you.
  - Shukran/afwan handle thanks.
</Summary>
