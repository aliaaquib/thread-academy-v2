Saying hello in Arabic opens doors everywhere. Arabic greetings are warm, musical and full of good wishes — people often ask after your health before anything else. In this chapter you will greet, introduce yourself and say goodbye like a native speaker.

This lesson focuses on Saying Hello: greet anyone, any time, with السلام عليكم and مرحبا.

<Definition term="Saying Hello">
  Greet anyone, any time, with السلام عليكم and مرحبا.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Greetings for the time of day

In the morning say صباح الخير (ṣabāḥ al-khayr, good morning) and in the evening مساء الخير (masāʾ al-khayr, good evening). The replies are just as pretty: صباح النور (ṣabāḥ an-nūr, morning of light) and مساء النور (masāʾ an-nūr, evening of light). Listen for which one people use and echo the pattern.

### Greetings come with good wishes

Arabic greetings love to wish people well. أهلا وسهلا (ahlan wa sahlan) means 'you have come to family and easy ground' — a warm welcome for guests. When someone asks how you are, answer بخير، الحمد لله (bi-khayr, al-ḥamdu li-llāh — well, praise be to God). These little phrases make every hello feel like a hug.

**Key term — السلام عليكم (as-salāmu ʿalaykum) — peace be upon you:** The all-purpose Arabic greeting, polite for every age and occasion. Answer it with وعليكم السلام (wa ʿalaykum as-salām, and peace be upon you).

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Saying Hello">
  How do you say 'hello' in a friendly, informal way?

  مرحبا (marḥaban).

  **Answer:** مرحبا (marḥaban).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying مع السلامة when you arrive** مع السلامة is only for leaving. When you arrive, say السلام عليكم or مرحبا.
  - **Replying to السلام عليكم with السلام عليكم** The matching reply is وعليكم السلام (wa ʿalaykum as-salām) — 'and peace be upon you too'. Repeating the greeting back sounds unfinished.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="What is the reply to السلام عليكم?" hint="It starts with و and returns the wish of peace.">
    وعليكم السلام (wa ʿalaykum as-salām).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="It is 8 p.m. Which greeting do you use?" hint="Evening = masāʾ.">
    مساء الخير (masāʾ al-khayr) — good evening.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="How do you say goodbye to a friend who is going home?" hint="It means 'with safety'.">
    مع السلامة (maʿa as-salāma).
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="How do you ask a girl 'what is your name?'" hint="Use the feminine ending ـكِ (ki).">
    ما اسمكِ؟ (mā ismuki?).
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
