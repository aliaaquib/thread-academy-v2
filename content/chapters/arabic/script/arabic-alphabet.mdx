## The Arabic Alphabet

Arabic reads right to left, letters join up, and most consonants come in four shapes. Learn the logic and the script unlocks fast.

<Definition term="Positional forms">
  Arabic letters take different **forms** — isolated, initial, medial, final — depending on neighbours. Six letters (ا د ذ ر ز و) don’t connect to the left, creating natural breaks.
</Definition>

## Key points

- 28 letters, written right to left.
- Short vowels are usually omitted in writing — context fills them in.
- Sun and moon letters: ‘al-’ assimilates before sun letters (ash-shams, not al-shams).
- Start with: ا ب ت م ن ل ك — the friendliest shapes.

<Example title="Reading مدرسة">
  مدرسة (madrasa = school): meem-da-ra-seen-ta marbuta. No short vowels written — but any Arabic reader sounds it out instantly.
</Example>
