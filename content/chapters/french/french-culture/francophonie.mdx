## Key ideas

### France debates itself

Laïcité shapes debates on religious symbols in schools; integration and identity are contested; young people face unemployment and housing pressures. The francophone world adds post-colonial perspectives — Senegalese or Québécois voices enrich any essay.

### Rituals of daily life

Meals structure the day: a light petit-déjeuner, a substantial déjeuner, and dîner around 8pm. La bise greets friends; politeness (bonjour, s'il vous plaît) is non-negotiable. Sundays remain family-centred, with many shops closed.

**Key term — la francophonie:** The French-speaking world — from Québec to Senegal to Vietnam, with hugely diverse cultures.

<WorkedExample title="Discussing laïcité">
  Write two balanced sentences giving an opinion on laïcité for a speaking exam.

  State an opinion: À mon avis, la laïcité protège l'égalité entre les citoyens.
  Add nuance: Cependant, certains pensent qu'elle limite la liberté religieuse.
  Balanced argument scores the highest marks.

  **Answer:** À mon avis, la laïcité protège l'égalité entre les citoyens. Cependant, certains pensent qu'elle limite la liberté religieuse.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Calling French culture 'just France'** The francophonie spans Africa, the Caribbean and beyond — mention it.
  - **Generalising about 'the French'** France is regionally diverse — Parisian habits are not universal.
</ImportantNote>
