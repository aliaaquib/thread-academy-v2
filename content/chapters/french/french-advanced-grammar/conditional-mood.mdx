  Say what you would do.

## Key ideas

### Conditionals and hypotheses

Present conditional: infinitive + imperfect endings (je parlerais). Si-clauses follow strict patterns: si + present → future (s'il pleut, je resterai); si + imperfect → conditional (s'il pleuvait, je resterais); si + pluperfect → past conditional (regrets). Never put the conditional after si.

### The subjunctive in action

Triggers: wanting (vouloir que), doubt (douter que), necessity (il faut que), emotion (avoir peur que), and conjunctions (pour que, avant que, bien que). Formation: take the 'ils' present stem and add -e, -es, -e, -ions, -iez, -ent (parler → que je parle); key irregulars: être → sois, avoir → aie, aller → aille.

**Key term — le conditionnel:** The conditional — je ferais (I would do). Formed from the infinitive + -ais endings, with irregular stems like ser-, aur-, ir-.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: The Conditional">
  Correct the error: 'si j'aurais su'.

  Si j'avais su — si never takes the conditional.

  **Answer:** Si j'avais su — si never takes the conditional.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Putting the conditional after 'si'** 'Si j'aurais' is the classic error — si takes the imperfect: si j'avais.
  - **Using the indicative after 'il faut que'** Il faut que tu fasses — necessity always takes the subjunctive.
</ImportantNote>
