## Key ideas

### Analysing poetry

Comment on versification (alexandrins — 12-syllable lines), rimes, rythme and images. Link technique to effect: Baudelaire's synaesthesia in 'Correspondances' fuses the senses to suggest a hidden unity — always connect device to meaning.

### Writing about film

Use technical terms: la mise en scène, le montage, la photographie. Structure essays with a thesis on the director's vision, analysis of key sequences, and a conclusion on the message. Context matters — the Nouvelle Vague reacted against studio convention.

**Key term — l'intrigue:** The plot — le dénouement (the resolution), le point culminant (the climax).

<WorkedExample title="Analysing an alexandrin">
  Baudelaire writes: 'La Nature est un temple où de vivants piliers…' Identify the device and its effect.

  Device: an extended metaphor — Nature as a temple.
  Effect: a sacred, mysterious atmosphere.
  Draft: Baudelaire emploie une métaphore filée comparant la Nature à un temple, ce qui confère au monde naturel une dimension sacrée et mystérieuse.
  Link: this announces the poem's theme of hidden correspondences.

  **Answer:** Baudelaire emploie une métaphore filée comparant la Nature à un temple, ce qui confère au monde naturel une dimension sacrée et mystérieuse, annonçant le thème des correspondances.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Summarising instead of analysing** Marks come from explaining how meaning is created, not from retelling the story.
  - **Quoting without analysis** Each quotation needs a comment on technique and effect.
</ImportantNote>
