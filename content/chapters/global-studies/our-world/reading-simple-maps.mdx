## Key ideas

### North, south, east and west

Compass directions help you describe where things are on a map. North is usually at the top of a map. You can also use words like 'near', 'next to' and 'between' to describe places in relation to one another.

### Maps are pictures of places

A map is a flat drawing that shows where places are. It cannot show everything, so it uses colours, labels and symbols to keep things clear. When you read a map, always start by looking at the title and the key, which explains what the colours and symbols mean.

**Key term — Continent:** One of the seven large land masses on Earth: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, South America and Oceania. A continent is the biggest division of land on a map.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Reading Simple Maps">
  Which direction on a map is usually at the top?

  North is usually at the top of a map.

  **Answer:** North is usually at the top of a map.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Thinking the equator is a real line painted on the Earth** The equator is an imaginary line drawn around the middle of the Earth on maps and globes. It helps people describe location, but you cannot see it on the ground.
  - **Saying England is the country when you mean the United Kingdom** England is one part of the United Kingdom, which also includes Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The country that has a seat at the United Nations is the United Kingdom.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="How many continents are there on Earth?" hint="The answer is a single digit.">
    Seven.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name three of the five oceans." hint="One of them starts with P and is the largest.">
    Any three of: the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic oceans.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is a capital city?" hint="Think about where a country's government usually works.">
    A capital city is the most important city in a country, usually where its government is based.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="On a map, what does the key (or legend) do?" hint="It explains something shown on the map.">
    The key explains what the colours, lines and symbols on the map mean.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
