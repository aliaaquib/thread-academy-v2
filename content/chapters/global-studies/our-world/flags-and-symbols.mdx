## Key ideas

### Every flag tells a story

A national flag is a special cloth design that stands for a country. Flags use colours and shapes that often have meanings, such as a colour that stands for the land or a symbol that stands for the country's history.

### Maps are pictures of places

A map is a flat drawing that shows where places are. It cannot show everything, so it uses colours, labels and symbols to keep things clear. When you read a map, always start by looking at the title and the key, which explains what the colours and symbols mean.

**Key term — Continent:** One of the seven large land masses on Earth: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, South America and Oceania. A continent is the biggest division of land on a map.

<WorkedExample title="Finding a country on a world map">
  Look at a world map. Point to the continent of Africa, then find the country of Egypt and say which ocean or sea is nearest to it.

  First, look for the labels of the seven continents and find 'Africa' in the middle of the map.
  Next, look at the smaller labels inside Africa and find the country called 'Egypt'.
  Look around Egypt for blue areas, which stand for water, and notice the Mediterranean Sea is just north of it.
  Check the map key to confirm that blue means sea or ocean.

  **Answer:** Egypt is a country in the north-east of Africa, and the nearest sea is the Mediterranean Sea.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying England is the country when you mean the United Kingdom** England is one part of the United Kingdom, which also includes Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. The country that has a seat at the United Nations is the United Kingdom.
  - **Thinking the equator is a real line painted on the Earth** The equator is an imaginary line drawn around the middle of the Earth on maps and globes. It helps people describe location, but you cannot see it on the ground.
</ImportantNote>
