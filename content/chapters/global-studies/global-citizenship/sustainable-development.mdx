## Sustainable Development

The Sustainable Development Goals are humanity’s shared to-do list — 17 goals, 169 targets, one deadline that’s already slipped.

<Definition term="Sustainable development">
  **Sustainable development** meets present needs without compromising future generations’ ability to meet theirs (Brundtland, 1987) — balancing economic, social and environmental goals.
</Definition>

## Key points

- The 17 SDGs: no poverty, zero hunger, health, education, gender equality, clean water, clean energy, decent work, innovation, reduced inequalities, sustainable cities, responsible consumption, climate action, life below water, life on land, peace and justice, partnerships.
- Logic: the goals interconnect — girls’ education cuts poverty AND improves health.
- Progress: real gains (extreme poverty halved before 2015’s baseline), but 2030 targets are mostly off-track.
- Criticism: too many goals, too vague, no enforcement — but they focus global attention and aid.

<Example title="Girls’ education: the super-goal">
  Educating girls correlates with lower child mortality, higher incomes and slower population growth. One goal accelerates half the others — that’s the SDG logic.
</Example>
