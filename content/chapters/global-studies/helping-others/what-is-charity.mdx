Kindness does not stop at your street or your country — it can travel across the whole world. This chapter is about helping others: what charities do, how aid reaches people after emergencies, and the small kind acts you can do yourself.

This lesson focuses on What Is Charity?: find out what charities do and why people give their time or money to help others.

<Definition term="What Is Charity?">
  Find out what charities do and why people give their time or money to help others.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Help can travel far

People in one country can help people in another. After a flood or earthquake, charities and governments send food, water and medicine to the people affected. This is called international aid, and it shows that the world can work together in hard times.

### Giving helps both sides

When you help someone, they get the help they need — and you feel good too. Scientists have found that doing kind acts can make people happier. Giving your time can be just as valuable as giving money.

**Key term — Charity:** An organisation set up to help people, animals or the environment. Charities collect donations of money, food or time and use them to do good work.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: What Is Charity?">
  What does a volunteer do?

  A volunteer gives their time to help others without being paid.

  **Answer:** A volunteer gives their time to help others without being paid.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Thinking only money counts as helping** Giving your time, skills or even a kind word can help just as much as money. Volunteering is a powerful form of help.
  - **Forgetting to check before you give** Always ask a trusted adult before donating money or joining a collection, and check that the charity is genuine. Safe giving is smart giving.
</ImportantNote>
