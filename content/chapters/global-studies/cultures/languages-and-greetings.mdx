## Key ideas

### Differences are worth respecting

No culture is better or worse than another; they are just different ways of living. Being respectful means listening, asking kind questions and not judging customs you do not understand. Curiosity and kindness help people from different cultures become friends.

### Food tells us about a place

What people eat often depends on what grows nearby and the climate they live in. People in hot countries may eat lots of fruit and rice, while people in cold countries may eat warming soups and bread. Trying new foods is a way of learning about another culture.

**Key term — Language:** The words and sounds a group of people use to communicate. There are thousands of languages spoken around the world.

<WorkedExample title="Connecting food to a place">
  Rice is a staple food in Japan, and bread is a staple food in France. Suggest one reason why the two countries eat different staple foods.

  Think about what 'staple food' means: the food people eat most often.
  Remember that people grow what suits their land and climate.
  Japan has lots of watery lowland fields that are perfect for growing rice.
  France has wide plains with a climate that suits growing wheat, which is made into bread.

  **Answer:** People eat what grows well in their land and climate — rice suits Japan's wet fields, while wheat for bread suits France's plains.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Thinking everyone in a country is exactly the same** Every country has many different cultures, foods and languages inside it. People in one city can live very differently from people in another city in the same country.
  - **Believing traditional dress is all anyone wears** Many people wear modern everyday clothes and only wear traditional dress for festivals, weddings or ceremonies.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Give one reason why people in different countries eat different foods." hint="It has to do with what grows nearby.">
    People eat what grows well in their climate and land, so foods differ from place to place.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is culture?" hint="Think about food, clothes, language and traditions.">
    Culture is the shared way of life of a group of people — their food, clothes, language, music and traditions.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name one way people can show respect for another culture." hint="Think about how you behave when you do not understand something.">
    Ask kind questions, listen and avoid judging customs you do not understand.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="True or false: everyone in one country eats the same food." hint="Think about how varied a single city can be.">
    False — most countries contain many different cultures and foods.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
