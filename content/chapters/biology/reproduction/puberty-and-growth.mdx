## Key ideas

### Puberty and the human life cycle

Humans pass through infancy, childhood, adolescence, adulthood and old age. During puberty, usually between about 10 and 16, hormones trigger physical changes: growth spurts, development of reproductive organs, and secondary sexual characteristics such as facial hair or breast development. Emotional changes are normal too, as the brain continues developing into the mid-twenties.

### Animal life cycles

Mammals give birth to live young that grow steadily into adults. Birds lay eggs that hatch into chicks. Amphibians such as frogs lay eggs in water that hatch into tadpoles, which gradually change into froglets. Insects such as butterflies undergo complete metamorphosis: egg → larva (caterpillar) → pupa (chrysalis) → adult. Each strategy suits the animal's habitat.

**Key term — adolescence:** The stage of life between childhood and adulthood when puberty occurs and the body becomes capable of reproduction.

</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Thinking metamorphosis means simply growing bigger** Metamorphosis is a complete rebuilding of the body form, not just growth. A tadpole does not simply inflate into a frog; it grows legs, loses its tail and changes how it breathes.
  - **Confusing pollination with fertilisation** Pollination is the transfer of pollen to the stigma; fertilisation is the later fusion of gametes inside the ovary. One is delivery, the other is joining.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="What is the difference between pollination and fertilisation?" hint="One moves pollen; the other fuses cells.">
    Pollination is the transfer of pollen from anther to stigma. Fertilisation is the fusion of male and female gametes to form a zygote.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name two ways insects help flowering plants reproduce." hint="Think about what attracts them.">
    Insects carry pollen between flowers when visiting for nectar; bright petals and scent attract them, and sticky or spiky pollen clings to their bodies.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="List the four stages of a butterfly's life cycle in order." hint="It starts with an egg.">
    Egg → larva (caterpillar) → pupa (chrysalis) → adult.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="How does a frog's life cycle differ from a human's?" hint="Think about where the young live.">
    Frogs lay eggs in water that hatch into tadpoles, which live in water and undergo metamorphosis into air-breathing froglets. Human babies are born live and grow steadily without metamorphosis.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
