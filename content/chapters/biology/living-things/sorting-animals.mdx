## Key ideas

### The seven life processes

Scientists remember the seven life processes with MRS GREN: Movement, Respiration, Sensitivity, Growth, Reproduction, Excretion and Nutrition. Every living thing carries out all seven. A car moves but does not grow or reproduce, so it is not alive; a mushroom grows, respires and reproduces, so it is.

### Habitats and adaptation

A habitat is an organism's home. A pond, a woodland and a garden wall are all habitats. Living things suit their habitats: a frog's moist skin helps it live near water, while a cactus stores water to survive in a desert. When a habitat changes, the organisms living there must cope or move.

**Key term — organism:** A single living thing, such as a daisy, a sparrow or a person.

<WorkedExample title="Alive, once alive, or never alive?">
  Sort a flame, a mushroom, a wooden chair and a puppy into three groups: alive now, once alive, or never alive.

  Check each one against the seven life processes (MRS GREN).
  The puppy moves, breathes, senses, grows, and will one day reproduce, so it is alive.
  The mushroom grows, respires and reproduces using spores, so it is alive too.
  The wooden chair came from a tree, which was alive, but the chair itself carries out none of the life processes.
  The flame moves and seems to grow, but it does not sense, excrete or reproduce like a living thing.

  **Answer:** Alive now: puppy and mushroom. Once alive: wooden chair. Never alive: flame.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Drawing food chain arrows backwards** Arrows point from the food to the animal eating it (grass → rabbit → fox), because they show where the energy is going.
  - **Calling anything that moves alive** Wind, flames and cars move but carry out none of the other life processes. Movement alone does not make something alive.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="A fish and a fishing rod both come from the water. Which is alive and why?" hint="One carries out life processes; the other never did.">
    The fish is alive because it carries out all seven life processes. The fishing rod was never alive and carries out none of them.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name a habitat near your home and one animal that lives there." hint="Think of gardens, ponds, parks or walls.">
    Example: a garden pond is a habitat for frogs, which need the water to keep their skin moist and to lay their eggs.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Name three of the seven life processes." hint="Think of MRS GREN.">
    Any three of: movement, respiration, sensitivity, growth, reproduction, excretion, nutrition.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Is a mushroom alive? Explain your answer." hint="Check it against the life processes.">
    Yes. A mushroom grows, respires to release energy, and reproduces by releasing spores, so it carries out the life processes.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
