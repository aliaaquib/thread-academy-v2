## Key ideas

### What plants need

Plants need light, water, warmth and air to grow well. Without enough light, stems grow tall, pale and weak as they stretch towards it. Without enough water, leaves droop and wilt. Gardeners test one need at a time, keeping everything else the same, so they know exactly what makes the difference.

### The jobs of plant parts

Roots anchor the plant and take in water and minerals. The stem holds the plant upright and carries water up to the leaves. Leaves are the food factories where photosynthesis happens. Flowers make seeds so new plants can grow. Each part has a job, and the plant suffers if any part is damaged.

**Key term — photosynthesis:** The process by which green plants use light energy to make food (glucose) from carbon dioxide and water.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: What Plants Need">
  List three things a plant needs to grow healthily.

  Any three of: light, water, warmth, air (carbon dioxide).

  **Answer:** Any three of: light, water, warmth, air (carbon dioxide).
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Saying plants eat soil** Plants absorb water and minerals from soil, but they make their own food in their leaves using sunlight. Soil is not their food.
  - **Forgetting that roots grow first** During germination the root appears before the shoot, so the young plant can absorb water straight away.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="In the fair test example, why must both plants get the same amount of water?" hint="What would happen if only one got water?">
    So that water cannot explain any difference in growth. If only one plant were watered, you would not know whether light or water caused the result.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What happens during germination?" hint="What comes out of the seed first?">
    The seed starts to grow: the root emerges first to absorb water, then the shoot grows upwards towards the light.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="What is the job of the roots?" hint="Think about water and holding on.">
    Roots anchor the plant in the soil and absorb water and minerals.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Why are green plants called producers?" hint="What do they make that animals cannot?">
    Because they produce their own food by photosynthesis, so they are the starting point of food chains.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
